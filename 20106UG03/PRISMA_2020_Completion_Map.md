## What is complete

The academic evidence collection has 101 distinct DOI records, 101 distinct PDF hashes, verified journal bibliography, 101 written summaries, a consolidated Word file and a structured review draft. This exceeds the requested 100-paper collection count.

## What this does not complete

It does not complete an exhaustive systematic review, independent human screening, formal bias appraisal, author declarations or journal submission. A journal may accept a deliberately limited structured-review design, but that suitability cannot be assumed. The draft must not be submitted under a false claim of full PRISMA compliance.
