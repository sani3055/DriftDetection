## Main findings

Coupling does not yet demonstrate a consistent detection improvement. Encoding
and score cutoff choices substantially change the result. Zero false positives
can coincide with zero attack recall. Historical candidate guards do not ensure
future safety, and predicted normal candidate rows can contain labelled attacks.
The seven field representation collapses the labelled test corpus to 123
profiles, sixteen of which contain both labels. This supports investigating
behavioural context, not claiming that more explanation statistics alone solve
the task.
