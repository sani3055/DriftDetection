As M L becomes increasingly integrated into cybersecurity systems, th e eth ical implications of AI-driven security

M L h as emerged as a transformative force in th e field of cybersecurity, oﬀering scalable, adaptive, and intelligent

and act upon model predictions. Edge AI and ligh tweigh t learning arch itectures will play a critical role in enabling low-
