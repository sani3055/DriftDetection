# Human screening instructions

For each selected record, two reviewers should independently complete the following fields:

1. title/abstract include, exclude, or unclear;
2. full-text retrieved yes/no;
3. full-text include/exclude;
4. exclusion reason selected from the protocol;
5. dataset and temporal resolution;
6. label source and label quality;
7. detector family and baselines;
8. drift signal and adaptation policy;
9. explanation method;
10. metrics, external validation, and reproducibility evidence;
11. risk-of-bias judgment and supporting note.

Disagreements must be resolved by discussion or a third reviewer. The current CSV contains metadata
and automated screening only; it is not a substitute for this review step.
